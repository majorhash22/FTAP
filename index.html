The Community Code

Good teammates.
Better people.

A little respect goes a long way.
In the game. In the server. In real life.

  1. Respect the person.

    Treat everyone like a teammate.

  2. Compete without the toxicity.

    Play to win. Keep personal attacks out.

  3. Keep it in the right channel.

    Give conversations and game invites the right home.

  4. Protect each other's privacy.

    Ask before sharing private messages or photos.

  5. Skip the spam.

    No scams, unsolicited promotions, or repeated pings.

  6. Let real life come first.

    Nobody owes the queue their time.
